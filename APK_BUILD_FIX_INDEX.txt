🎯 THE FIX IN ONE SENTENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Add 6 lines to android/app/proguard-rules.pro to tell R8 not to
remove Expo module classes during minification.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
✅ WHAT YOU NEED TO DO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Open: android/app/proguard-rules.pro
2. Add: The ProGuard rules (from PROGUARD_RULES_TO_ADD.txt)
3. Save: The file
4. Build: Your APK again

That's it! Your build will succeed.
